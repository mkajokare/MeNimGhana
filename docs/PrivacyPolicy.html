Privacy notice

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This notice explains what MeNim Ghana does with the information you give us when you join the launch list on this page. When the full website opens, a fuller privacy policy will replace it.

Who we are

MeNim Ghana (“we”) runs menimghana.com, a business directory for Ghana. You can contact us about this notice at support@menimghana.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Whether you told us you are a business owner or a visitor.
  • The date and time you joined.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this page. Like any website, the services that deliver the page (listed below) receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads it.

Why we use it

  • To email you when MeNim Ghana goes live, and to tell you about the launch.
  • To see how many business owners and how many visitors are interested, so we can plan the launch.

We ask for your consent when you join, and we use your email address only for these purposes. We do not sell it and we do not give it to other companies to use for their own marketing.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the launch list in our database.
  • GitHub hosts this website (GitHub Pages).
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr supply the fonts and a code library the page uses, so they receive your IP address when the page loads.

Some of these companies operate outside Ghana, so your information may be handled in other countries.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it for the launch. Every email we send you will include a way to opt out.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent whenever you like. Email support@menimghana.com and we will deal with your request. You may also complain to Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top of this page.
